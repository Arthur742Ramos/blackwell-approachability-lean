# Third-party notices

The project depends on the pinned Mathlib Lake package recorded in
`lake-manifest.json`. Mathlib and its transitive dependencies retain their
upstream licenses and notices; they are not copied into this repository.

The selected theorem statements formalize the explicit construction in:

- C. Dann, Y. Mansour, M. Mohri, J. Schneider, and B. Sivan,
  “Rate-Preserving Reductions for Blackwell Approachability,” *Proceedings of
  the Thirty Eighth Conference on Learning Theory*, PMLR 291, 1380–1414
  (2025), <https://proceedings.mlr.press/v291/dann25a.html>.

The repository follows the cited construction and does not claim mathematical
priority. Earlier local support modules also draw on D. Blackwell, “An analog
of the minimax theorem for vector payoffs,” *Pacific Journal of Mathematics* 6
(1956), 1–8.
